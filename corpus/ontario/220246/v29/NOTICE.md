Ontario e-Laws dates v29 from July 1 through July 31, 2025, inclusive. The
preceding [v28](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/220246/v28) ends June 30.

To reproduce the excerpt, take the historical notice and the paragraphs for
subsections (2) and (3) under the BK129 anchor in the English v29 HTML. Strip
HTML tags and outer whitespace and decode HTML entities. Join subsection (2)
and its clauses (a) and (b) with one LF between paragraphs, keeping the ordinary
space after each clause label. Join the notice and the two subsection blocks
with one blank line between blocks. Write UTF-8 with LF and one final newline,
then recompute the SHA-256 of those bytes. Each citation span starts after its
subsection label and ends before its amendment reference. The November 27,
2026 download reproduced both blocks and the historical notice from the
November 22 capture recorded in the manifest.
